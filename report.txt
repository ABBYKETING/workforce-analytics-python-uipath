PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project investigates relationships between
education, employment outcomes, income patterns,
and workplace satisfaction using three datasets.

Analysis tools: Python, pandas, NumPy,
Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Jupyter Notebook.
Automation platform: UiPath Studio.


1. GRADUATE EMPLOYMENT SURVEY
--------------------------------------------------

The cleaned graduate employment dataset contains
4,656 records.

Average overall employment rate: 91.76%
Average full-time permanent employment rate: 81.95%
Mean of reported programme-level median salaries:
SGD 3,584.05

Key observations:
- Starting salaries vary across schools and fields.
- Computing, law and medicine-related groups
  generally show stronger salary outcomes.
- Employment rates and permanent employment
  rates differ across degree fields.


2. MOM LABOUR FORCE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------

Key observations:
- Managers and administrators have comparatively
  high median monthly incomes.
- Cleaners, labourers and related workers have
  comparatively low median monthly incomes.
- Gender differences are visible across occupations.
- Degree holders generally earn higher median
  incomes than lower qualification groups.
- Income patterns vary across age and qualification.

These findings are descriptive and do not establish
causal relationships.


3. GLASSDOOR EMPLOYEE SATISFACTION
--------------------------------------------------

Records analysed: 838,566

Average overall satisfaction:
Current employees: 3.81 / 5
Former employees: 3.44 / 5

Average senior management satisfaction:
Current employees: 3.29 / 5
Former employees: 2.94 / 5

CORRELATION WITH OVERALL RATING

Senior management:       0.6843
Career opportunities:    0.6610
Compensation & benefits: 0.5467
Work-life balance:       0.5217
Employee recommendation: 0.6276

Key findings:
- Current employees generally report higher
  satisfaction than former employees.
- Senior management and career opportunities show
  relatively strong positive correlations with
  overall satisfaction.
- Employees with higher satisfaction ratings are
  more likely to recommend their workplace.
- Correlation does not imply causation.


4. AUTOMATION COMPONENT
--------------------------------------------------

UiPath workflow components:

Download Dataset.xaml
- Interacts with browser download controls
  for Kaggle and MOM statistical resources.

Reporting.xaml
- Starts the Python graph-generation script.
- Reads report.txt.
- Displays report text in a message box.
- Contains an Outlook desktop email activity.

The current workflow requires local paths and
email configuration before it can be reused.


5. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------

Education and employment characteristics are
associated with differences in income and
employment outcomes.

Workplace satisfaction is positively associated
with perceptions of management, career development,
and employee recommendation behaviour.

The project demonstrates practical experience in
data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis,
statistical visualisation, and RPA workflow design.
